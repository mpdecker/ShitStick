# The Shit-Covered Stick

*A student asked Yúnmén: "What is Buddha?"*
*Yúnmén said: "A shit-covered stick."*

---

You have found it. Or it has found you. The distinction does not matter.

This is not an app. An app has buttons. An app wants something from you. This wants nothing. It will give you a koan — one word, one question, one impossible statement on a black screen — and then it will be silent for hours. Or days. Or weeks. It does not care how long. Neither should you.

## What It Does

It shows you one koan.

Then it waits.

When the time is ripe — not when you are ready, not when you have solved it, not when you have forgotten it, not when you remember it — another koan arrives.

You do not choose the timing. The timing is not chosen. It arises.

## What It Does Not Do

There is no progress bar.

There is no streak.

There is no history of past koans, no archive, no way to scroll back. The koan that came before is gone. If you grasped it, let go. If you never understood it, good.

There are no settings. The masters did not offer settings.

## On Installation

Put it on your phone. Forget you put it on your phone. This is the correct sequence.

## On Notifications

At some point your phone will vibrate or chime. You will look down expecting something important. You will read eight words. You will not understand them. You will put your phone away.

This is the practice.

## On Understanding

A student studied for seven years and came to the master.

"I have not yet understood," said the student.

"Good," said the master. "Keep going."

## Technical Notes

*For those who cannot resist looking behind the screen.*

Built with Flutter. Koans are scheduled as local notifications with deliberately irregular delays — weighted toward medium gaps, with a long tail extending to weeks. The irregularity is not a bug. Do not file an issue asking for a settings screen. There will not be a settings screen.

The black screen is not a placeholder. It is the point.

## Contributing

If you have a koan that belongs here, open a pull request. The criteria: it must have stopped you. Not puzzled you — stopped you. If you kept walking after reading it, it is not ready.
